
Accessibility rule (MANDATORY):
- Do NOT use ANSI colour escape codes (e.g. \033[31m, \e[32m, tput setaf) for
  emphasis or to highlight text.
- Use bold formatting instead where available (e.g. \033[1m ... \033[0m), or
  plain text if the context does not support bold.
- This ensures output remains readable for users with colour-blindness or on
  terminals that do not support colour.
